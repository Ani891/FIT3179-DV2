Australians took 17.3 million caravan and camping trips in 2025, and the number of nights away keeps climbing. The headline hides who goes, why they go and where.

This page follows caravan and camping travel across the whole country, from national totals to state and regional patterns, using Tourism Research Australia figures. It asks one question: why does road travel remain such a popular way to see Australia?

TripOne journey away from home that includes at least one night.
NightOne night spent away from home during a trip.
Year to June 2026A 12-month period ending in June 2026. It overlaps with calendar 2025, so the two are not separate years.

How big is the habit?

Start with the national picture. Caravan and camping travel is large, it is mostly a regional business, and most of the money goes to commercial parks.

Who goes and for how long

The stereotype is a retiree on a long trip. The numbers show a mix of couples, families, short breaks and long journeys, and mostly holidays.

Where Australians go

Every state has its own pattern. These charts and maps use 2024 figures, the latest with a full set of states and regions.

The road is still calling.

The habit is growingNights away rose from 57.9 million in 2025 to 60.8 million in the year to June 2026.
It is a regional story87% of caravan and camping nights in 2025 were spent in regional Australia.
Short breaks and long journeysIn 2024, 45% of trips were 2 to 3 nights, yet trips of 15 nights or more made up 18% of all nights.
